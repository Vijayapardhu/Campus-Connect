# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, disability, appearance, ethnicity,
gender identity, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind. Assume the person you are replying to is doing their best.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience. Everyone was new once,
  and "obvious" is relative.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, apologise, and move on.
- Respect that maintainers are volunteers with other commitments. A slow reply
  is not a slight.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberately derailing threads.
- Dismissing or belittling someone for not knowing something.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these rules, report it privately
by opening a
[security advisory](https://github.com/Vijayapardhu/Campus-Connect/security/advisories/new)
— it is the private channel this repository has available — or by contacting the
maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.

Reports are handled confidentially. You will not be asked to justify why
something upset you.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that break these
rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge
to be inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
